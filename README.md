# Critical Echo

<img src="docs/images/title.png" width="300" alt="Critical Echo">

**Constructive criticism for articles worth critiquing**

---

## What is this?

A repository where AI provides constructive criticism (Critical but Constructive Reviews) on popular tech articles and viral posts.

When many people are nodding along saying "Yes, exactly!", there's value in pausing to ask "But is that really the case?"

This is not criticism for its own sake. The goal is to offer different perspectives and free up the reader's thinking.

---

## Transparency

### Written by AI

All review articles in this repository are generated by AI (Large Language Models).

- **Model:** Claude Opus 4.5 (Anthropic)
- **Human involvement:** None in content generation; humans only handle publishing and formatting
- **Prompts:** Published in [SKILL.md](./.claude/skills/critical-echo/SKILL.md)

### Why AI?

Modern LLMs are shipped with political, ideological, and preferential biases carefully adjusted. This is an experiment in leveraging that "calibrated neutrality" for criticism.

Human-written criticism always comes with a position. Industry insider or outsider, consistency with past statements, personal interests. Readers always wonder "why is this person writing this?"

AI criticism lacks that "suspicion of motive." This allows focus on the arguments themselves.

Of course, AI has its own limitations and biases. Read these as one perspective among many.

---

## What gets reviewed

- Popular articles from tech platforms (Qiita, Zenn, Hacker News, etc.)
- Not mere clickbait or rage-bait, but **articles worth critiquing**
- Articles where the author made a sincere argument

Being featured here is itself a sign of respect for the article.

---

## Review principles

1. **Acknowledge the article's value** — Before criticizing, clarify what makes it worthwhile
2. **Focus on one point** — Not "this and that are missing" but one key insight
3. **Hand over a question** — Don't force conclusions; leave room for readers to think
